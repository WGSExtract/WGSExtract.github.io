Sex, relatedness and ancestry (qc relate)

wgsextract qc relate answers three questions with somalier (Pedersen et al. 2020, Genome Medicine):

  • Is this file really mine? It infers the sex from chrX and chrY and checks it against the sex already recorded for the file.
  • Are these kits from relatives? It estimates the relatedness of every pair of inputs: about 1 for the same person, 0.5 for parent and child or full siblings, 0.25 for second-degree relatives and about 0 for unrelated people.
  • Which continental group? It predicts one of the five 1000 Genomes superpopulations (AFR, AMR, EAS, EUR, SAS). This is a coarse continental estimate, not an ancestry breakdown.
pixi install -e somalier                 # once: the optional somalier tool
wgsextract ref somalier --install        # once: sites and 1000 Genomes sketches (~85 MB)
wgsextract qc relate me.cram
wgsextract qc relate child.cram mother.cram father.cram --ped trio.ped
wgsextract qc relate me.cram somalier/cousin.somalier

Inputs#

BAM, CRAM and VCF files, and .somalier sketches from an earlier run. Each BAM, CRAM or VCF is sketched at about 17,800 common SNPs (a few seconds for a VCF, a few minutes for a 30x CRAM). The reference is found from the file's header, as for other commands, or given with --ref. The sketches are kept in <outdir>/somalier/: they are a few hundred kilobytes, so a relative can send theirs instead of a whole genome.

somalier picks the sites file that matches each input's build and contig names (hg38, hg19/GRCh37 and T2T-CHM13). Kits on different builds can be compared. --sites uses your own sites VCF instead, for example for an exome.

A BAM or CRAM gives the most accurate results. A VCF's genotypes are used as called (REF and the site's ALT only; filtered, missing or other-allele calls are unknown). A VCF lists only variants, so a site it lacks is usually homozygous reference, but only in a whole-genome call set:

  • For a sample of a whole-genome, variants-only VCF (single-sample or joint-called), missing autosomal sites are written into its sketch as homozygous reference. Such a sketch can be reused, or compared with BAMs, without changing the result. "Whole genome" means non-reference calls on at least 95% of the autosomes, at least 80% of the variant sites the sites' allele frequencies predict, and at least 400 records per autosomal megabase in the whole file. Whole-genome VCFs reach 89-100% of the sites (GIAB HG002/HG003/HG004 v4.2.1 98%, 1000 Genomes NA19240 89% and HG00513 92%); HG002's chromosomes 1-6 alone reach 38% and its chr20 3%. somalier's sites suit exomes, so HG002 in the xGen exome (+-100 bp) still reaches 90%; its 25 records per megabase, against 1,408 for the whole VCF, tell it apart.
  • For any other variants-only VCF (one chromosome, an exome or panel), missing sites stay unknown. The sample's relatedness then rests on its variant sites alone, so its pairs are reported as uncertain, with a vcf_called_sites_only warning.
  • An all-sites VCF writes its hom-ref calls out, so they are used as they are, and its missing sites stay unknown, as a BAM's uncovered sites do; no warning.

chrX and chrY are never filled, so the sex reads only real calls. gVCFs are refused: their no-call blocks can't be told from reference blocks.

Sample names come from the read group (SM; else the file name) or the VCF header, and for a sketch from the name stored inside it, so a renamed .somalier file still matches its pedigree row. A BAM/CRAM of several samples, and names with path separators, are refused. When a BAM, CRAM or VCF has the sample name of another input, it is prefixed with its file name.

Outputs#

In --outdir, else beside the first input (for sketches in an earlier run's somalier/ folder, in that run's folder):

FileContents
relate.samples.tsvOne row per sample: inferred sex and its confidence, the sex recorded before, the superpopulation and its probability, depths and chrX heterozygosity
relate.pairs.tsvOne row per pair: relatedness, likely relationship, IBS0/IBS2, sites shared, expected relatedness from --ped, and whether it is flagged
relate.html, relate.ancestry.htmlsomalier's interactive reports
somalier/<sample>.somalierThe sketches
relate.manifest.jsonThe result manifest

--format parquet writes the two tables as Parquet.

Warnings#

CodeMeaning
sex_mismatchsomalier's sex disagrees with the sex recorded for the file: your override, a sex detected earlier, or for a BAM its chrX/chrY read counts (a CRAM is compared only once info or another command has recorded its sex)
vcf_called_sites_onlyA VCF sample isn't a whole-genome variant call set: its pairs are uncertain
same_personTwo inputs are the same person
relationship_mismatchWith --ped: a pair is more than 0.1 away from the relatedness the pedigree implies, for example a swapped file
ped_sex_mismatchWith --ped: a sample's sex disagrees with the pedigree

--ped takes a PED or PLINK .fam file (family, sample, father, mother, sex), tab- or space-separated, naming the samples as qc relate does.

The sex and superpopulation of each BAM or CRAM are recorded in its sample workspace as somalier_sex and superpopulation.

Resources and licences#

ref somalier --install downloads, and checks against pinned SHA-256 digests:

  • somalier's sites files (MIT), from the brentp/somalier release attachments;
  • ancestry-labels-1kg.tsv (MIT), from the somalier repository;
  • 1kg.somalier.tar.gz, sketches of the 2,504 unrelated 1000 Genomes samples from the NYGC high-coverage data, from Zenodo record 3479773 (CC BY 4.0, Brent Pedersen).

They go in <library>/somalier/. Each sketch's SHA-256 is recorded when the archive is unpacked and checked before every use (well under a second); ref somalier --delete removes them. Ancestry runs when they are installed, unless --sites gives another panel (--no-ancestry skips it).

Platforms#

somalier is built for Linux and macOS (x86-64 and ARM64) and is not available on Windows; use WSL2 there.
