New gates need an owner, an incident or concrete threat model, a measurable cost, narrow scope and a dated keep/narrow/drop review. At the first review, compare approval wait-hours, trivial review rounds, stale certification attempts and runner minutes, fallback rates, ready-to-runner p50/p90, and missed regressions by area. Separate genuine late fixes from routine supersession; the target for the latter is under 5% of attempts. Keep safety gates until an owner approves a replacement.
