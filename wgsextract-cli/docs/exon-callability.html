Exon callability (vcf callability)

A clinical findings list is silent about most genes, and silence can mean three different things:

  • not reported: the assay read every coding base of the gene and found nothing to report;
  • not readable: the assay could not read some of it, so a variant there would have been missed;
  • not run: no analysis looked at it.

Gene-body coverage can't tell these apart. A gene with reads over 90% of its body can still hide a whole coding exon nobody read, and 90% of a body that is mostly intron says little about its exons. vcf callability therefore classifies every coding exon of a gene list, base by base, from the sample's reference evidence (a gVCF), and calls a gene callable only when every one of its coding exons is. The personal report combines the result with the ClinVar section.

Callability is technical, not clinical. It says whether this sequencing run read the bases. A callable exon with nothing reported does not rule out disease, and no gene is "negative" while an exon, a paralog region or a variant class (below) remains unassessed.

wgsextract vcf snp --input sample.cram --reference-evidence gvcf
wgsextract vcf callability --vcf-input snps.vcf.gz
wgsextract vcf clinvar --vcf-input bcftools.g.vcf.gz --ref reference.fa
wgsextract report --input sample.cram

Inputs#

  • A single-sample gVCF. --vcf-input is a gVCF, or a VCF whose caller also wrote one (--reference-evidence gvcf with vcf snp, vcf indel, vcf gatk, vcf deepvariant or vcf clair3), found through its result manifest. A VCF without one stops with the command that makes it: a variant-only VCF can't tell a confident reference base from one nobody read. Both files must name exactly one sample and agree on its name. The caller's --region limits what was assessed; exons outside it are not_run.
  • A gene list. --gene-list takes the report's lists (default acmg-sf-3.3, or config report_gene_list) or a TSV/BED of symbols. Only the symbols are used, so nothing is downloaded.
  • An exon model. Bundled for hg38 and hg19 (below); --exons gives another (format below). Genes the model lacks are not_run (no_exon_model).

Bundled exon models#

assets/report/exons_hg38.tsv.gz and exons_hg19.tsv.gz list, for each gene of the built-in lists (ACMG SF v3.3 and v3.2, 84 genes, 1,852 exons), every exon of one transcript, numbered in transcript order, with its coding part:

scripts/build_report_exons.py builds both files from the pinned sources (each checked against its SHA-256; the file headers record the URLs and checksums). NCBI RefSeq annotation is public domain; the GIAB stratifications are NIST data freely available for reuse. The files hold coordinates computed from those sources and nothing else: no interpretation, classification or gene-list table from another tool is copied. The gene symbols are the ACMG SF lists' (facts, cited in the report).

States#

Each coding exon is assessed over its CDS plus --flank intronic bases on each side (default 2, the splice-site dinucleotides; up to 50). Each base gets the gVCF's evidence state with the confidence_* thresholds (--confidence-depth, --confidence-gq). A base is read when it is callable_reference or inside a small-variant call that passes the depth threshold and, when the evidence model supports it, the genotype-quality threshold, with all carried alleles resolved to sequence. A carried call that fails these checks is low_quality_variant, not readable. Bases in known short-read limitations are not counted as read.

Exon statusMeaning
callableevery base read
partialsome bases read, others not
not_readableno base read: no reads (uncovered), too few or too poor (low_quality_reference), or failed calls (filtered, no_call)
specialist_requiredoverlaps a short-read limitation: short reads can't place reads there uniquely, whatever the gVCF says, so a paralog-aware method (long reads, a gene-specific assay) is needed
not_runthe caller did not assess these bases (not_assessed)
not_applicableploidy 0 for this sample (excluded_by_policy, such as chrY in a woman); left out of every count

An exon's reasons list the states and limitations of its unread bases. The specialist field names a follow-up caller where one is known for that gene and build, such as long-read typing paraphase --genes pms2. This is the same guidance used for specialist_caller_required in typing target-cnv. Neither a copy-number screen nor a phased-allele result changes the small-variant callability measured from the gVCF.

Gene stateMeaning
callableevery coding exon callable
partialsome coding exons not callable
not_readableno coding exon callable or partial
not_runno exon model, or no coding exon assessed

In the report, each gene's result combines its state with the ClinVar section:

ResultMeaning
reportedthe ClinVar section lists a consensus P/LP call in the gene (with its confidence)
not_reportedno call, and every coding exon was read
not_readableno call, but not every coding exon was read
not_runvcf clinvar has not run, the gene is not in its gene list, or the gene was not assessed; also every gene without a call when the two runs can't be combined (warning clinvar_not_combined). Membership is by the report's own gene list symbols

Run vcf clinvar on the assessed gVCF itself. Annotating a variant-only companion such as snps.vcf.gz can omit indels. The report compares the gVCF fingerprints recorded by both runs and suppresses not_reported if they don't match, the builds differ, or ClinVar findings could not all be placed in genes.

What a callable exon does not cover#

A callable gene still leaves these unassessed (the JSON's unassessed_variant_classes):

  • exon or gene deletions and duplications and other structural variants (vcf sv, vcf cnv);
  • repeat expansions longer than a read (typing str);
  • promoters, untranslated regions and deep intronic variants;
  • exons only in other transcripts;
  • mosaic and low-fraction variants;
  • paralog regions (specialist_required exons).

Builds#

The gVCF's ##contig lengths must match the exon model's (#contig lines): a bundled model is chosen by them, and a mismatch (another build, such as T2T, or a model for another build) stops the run, naming the contigs that differ. Contig names may differ by chr (and M/MT).

Outputs#

  • exon_callability.json (role exon_callability, schema wgsextract.exon_callability/1): the gene list, the exon model (file, SHA-256, transcripts and source lines), the reference identity (build, the contigs checked, the FASTA's name), the evidence (the gVCF, relative to the document; engine and version, quality model, minimum mapping quality, assessed region), the thresholds, counts, and per gene its state, reasons, exons per status, the exon numbers not callable (most severe status first), its suggested specialist if any, and its assessed, read and limited bases.
  • exon_callability.tsv (role exon_callability_table): one row per coding exon with its status, reasons, assessed region, bases per evidence state, read and limited bases, limitation intervals, and the suggested specialist on applicable exons.

The result manifest records the gVCF as an input (vcf_input when given directly, reference_evidence when found as a companion), the metrics genes_<state> and exons_<status>, and the warnings exons_not_callable and genes_without_exon_model.

Exon model format#

A TSV (optionally gzipped) with #contig<TAB>name<TAB>length lines for every contig it uses, other # lines as its source notes, and the columns gene, transcript, tag, strand, exon, exons, chrom, start, end, cds_start, cds_end (1-based, inclusive, in order and within the exon; both CDS columns empty for an untranslated exon) and limitations (kind:start-end items separated by ;, may be empty). Each gene's rows must be one transcript's exons, numbered 1 to its exons count, each once; a model missing an exon is refused.

Measured on HG002#

HG002 (35x Illumina NovaSeq PCR-free, GRCh38 no-alt), vcf snp --reference-evidence gvcf over the ACMG SF v3.3 coding exons (±50 bp, 532 kb, 20 s on 3 threads), then vcf callability (under a second), defaults:

GenesCoding exons
callable701,735
partial14
specialist required88
not readable, not run00

Every one of the 84 genes passes a gene-body rule: at least 90% of each body has reads (and at least 90% has 10 or more reads at MAPQ 20). Exon by exon, 14 genes have coding exons short reads can't place uniquely, among them the known paralog regions: PMS2 exons 1-5, 9 and 11-15 (PMS2CL and the PMS2P pseudogenes), ABCD1 exons 7-10, PTEN exons 5, 8 and 9 (PTENP1), MYH7 (MYH6) and the TTN exons 172-198. The gVCF alone called 14,097 of those exons' 14,429 bases confident reference; all 328 bases it called low-quality were in them. With ClinVar (release of 2026-11-15) the report lists MSH2 as reported (one low-confidence call), 69 genes as not reported and the 14 as not readable.
