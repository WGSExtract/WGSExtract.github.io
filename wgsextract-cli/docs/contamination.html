DNA contamination (qc contamination)

A BAM or CRAM can hold reads from more than one person: a mixed-up or contaminated sample at the lab, or two runs merged by mistake. Nothing fails, but another person's reads turn homozygous sites heterozygous, so genotypes, microarray exports, haplogroups and reports can be wrong without any sign. lineage mt-haplogroup --contamination (haplocheck) only sees mtDNA.

wgsextract qc contamination --input sample.cram
wgsextract qc contamination --input sample.bam --sites 10k   # faster, noisier

How it works#

VerifyBamID2 (Zhang et al. 2020, Genome Research 30:185) counts the reads' alleles at common SNPs and fits them to a mixture of two people whose allele frequencies come from the principal components of a reference panel. It needs no genotypes of the sample, and it works when the sample and the contaminant have different ancestries. Its FREEMIX is the estimated fraction of the reads from the second person.

The panels are the 1000 Genomes phase 3 SVD resources that ship in VerifyBamID2's Bioconda package: 100,000 sites (default) or 10,000 sites (--sites 10k), for GRCh37/hg19 and GRCh38. Contig names (chr1 or 1) are matched to the input. The Bioconda package is checksummed in pixi.lock; WGS Extract downloads or redistributes nothing itself. VerifyBamID2 is MIT licensed, and the 1000 Genomes data are freely usable.

Builds. T2T-CHM13 and other builds have no bundled panel, so the command stops with a message saying so. Build a panel from a multi-sample VCF on that build (verifybamid2 --RefVCF panel.vcf.gz --Reference ref.fa, at least 1,000 samples) and pass its prefix with --svd-prefix.

Platforms. Linux and macOS: pixi install -e verifybamid2. Bioconda has no Windows build, so qc contamination is not available on native Windows (use WSL2).

Outputs#

  • <stem>.contamination.selfSM: VerifyBamID2's report (FREEMIX, reads, mean depth).
  • <stem>.contamination.Ancestry: the sample's (and the contaminant's) position on the panel's first principal components.
  • The result manifest's metrics: freemix, freemix_sites (panel sites with reads), freemix_reads, freemix_depth, freemix_confidence, svd_panel and ancestry_pc1, ancestry_pc2.
  • In a sample workspace, FREEMIX is recorded as the contamination fact of the alignment, with high confidence from 5,000 covered sites at 4x or more, medium with fewer, and low below 1,000 or when VerifyBamID2's marker check had to be skipped.

A region subset (such as one chromosome) covers too few sites for VerifyBamID2's marker check. The check is then skipped, the estimate is still made, and a contamination_few_sites warning says it is rough.

What uses it#

When FREEMIX is at or above qc_max_contamination (default 0.03, in config.toml; 0 turns this off):

  • qc contamination itself warns sample_contamination;
  • microarray, the vcf callers and the haplogroup commands warn sample_contamination before writing genotypes from that alignment (genotype QC);
  • confidence labels of rare and clinical findings cap every call at moderate, with reason sample_contaminated, for VCFs in the same genome folder;
  • the personal report shows the estimate in the overview, warns there, and caps at moderate the haplogroups of runs that warned. Without an estimate it lists qc contamination among the missing steps.

Pipelines draw the line between 2% and 5%; gnomAD removed samples above 5%. 3% is a middle choice: clean 30x genomes measure well below 1% (see below), so a value above it is very unlikely to be noise.

Validation#

GIAB NovaSeq 30x GRCh38 BAMs (Google brain-genomics-public), chr20 only, VerifyBamID2 2.0.3, 100k-site panel.
